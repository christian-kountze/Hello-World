# Iowa Hawkeyes Football 2025 Season

## Project Title
*Iowa Hawkeyes Football: 2025 Season Recap*

## Description
Here is a short look at the **2025 Iowa Hawkeyes** football season under head coach **Kirk Ferentz**.
Iowa went **9–4** (6–3 Big Ten) and beat No. 14 Vanderbilt **34–27** in the ReliaQuest Bowl.
